Tejas Kumar is an AI Engineer at IBM in Berlin and the bestselling author of Fluent React (O'Reilly). He has spent over 25 years building for the web, with experience at Spotify, Vercel, Xata, and DataStax, and has spoken at conferences around the world since 2012. He hosts ConTejas Code and believes the best technology starts with curiosity, care, and community.
