Charlie Gerard is a senior research engineer at CrowdStrike, a published author and creative technologist. For over a decade, she has been exploring innovative technologies and pushing the boundaries of what can be done using JavaScript. From building brain-controlled web interfaces, to creating ML models for motion control, to hacking cars, she loves researching and experimenting with the web. She is also a runner, drummer and beginner mountaineer.
