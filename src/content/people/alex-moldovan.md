Alex is one of the founders and organizers of JSHeroes. He has been active in the local community in Cluj since 2015, has given numerous talks at local and international events over the years, and currently works as a UI Platform Engineer at together.ai.

In his spare time, he enjoys building Legos, travelling with his family, and writing blog posts on his old-fashioned [House Moldovan travel journal](https://housemoldovan.com) or on his hiking-in-Romania website, [pecreste.ro](https://pecreste.ro).
