title: "Is your site ready for agents? An introduction to AEO"
---

Whether you are a fan of it or not, users will likely find out about your product, learn how to use it, and troubleshoot issues with an agent by their side.

The success of your users and your product now relies on how well your content plays along with LLMs and AI products. I will walk you through how we turn Supabase documentation from zero to agent ready. By helping the robots that help the humans, you might end up helping the humans using your site too.
